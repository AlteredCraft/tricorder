# G-0002.01. Guided A/B vertical slice

Status: In progress
Goal: [G-0002](G-0002-thin-investigation-slice.md) — one usable handheld loop.
ADRs: [0010](../adrs/ADR-0010-device-owned-ab-lan-experiment.md), [0011](../adrs/ADR-0011-bounded-tcp-write-completion.md), [0012](../adrs/ADR-0012-live-prose-over-verified-summaries.md), [0013](../adrs/ADR-0013-local-first-speech-to-text.md); reuses 0003–0009.
Revision: 2026-09-22: the comparison must be an instrument view, not only text (build step 2). The 1000 Hz tone fixture is retired for acoustic trials; transport changes are tested with SD replay and fault injection.
Revision: 2026-09-24: A and B come from the operator's question and the guidance, not a fixed fixture script; measurement stability is checked by repeating A inside the rated live loops, not by a separate lab fixture (user decision: usefulness first). Build step 1 and check 4 changed.

## Hypothesis

One explicit-turn A/B sound investigation on the Tab5, guided by the Mac service (mock first, then a live provider), produces a useful, evidence-grounded comparison without the operator touching the Mac during the loop. The existing instrument code can support it and be hardened in place.

**Refuted if:** the operator can't finish without developer help, guidance cites the wrong capture, changed settings make the comparison misleading, network waits block local cancel, or the operator can't understand the result or pick a next action.

## Build

1. Fixed capture settings (rate, gain, length, slot) as the evidence contract. What A and B are comes from the operator's question and the guidance. A broadband lab fixture is a fallback if repeats show the measurement is unstable.
2. On-device flow: ask → capture A → guidance → adjust → capture B → comparison, with recording/waiting/offline/cancelled/incomplete states visible. Instrument view: live spectrum while recording, A/B spectra overlaid in the comparison, a camera context shot, and an IMU steadiness indicator. Setup (Wi-Fi, endpoint) stays off the main flow. Spoken input and guidance, plus text.
3. Mac mock service and device transport. The protocol is in [guided-ab-protocol.md](../guided-ab-protocol.md).
4. Swap in a live model and speech provider behind the same contract. Keys stay on the Mac.
5. Handheld runs, fix blocking defects, review usefulness with the user.

Out of scope: full Explore/Compare breadth, all sensors at once, streaming, calibrated acoustics, hosted deployment, full-duplex speech.

## Checks

1. Host tests cover the state sequence, stale/duplicate/wrong-session rejection, delay, cancel, disconnect and capacity limits.
2. Three mock handheld loops, each with two real captures, guidance, the recorded adjustment and a comparison joined to those exact captures.
3. Controlled trials: 5 s delayed reply, cancel while pending, service disconnect/reconnect. The UI stays live and old-session replies can't act.
4. **Three live-provider handheld loops** on comparisons the operator cares about, each with capture/transfer/inference/first-response timing and one repeat of A (A-to-A change reported next to B/A). The operator rates responsiveness and usefulness 1–5 (target ≥ 4), states the finding and names a next action unaided.
