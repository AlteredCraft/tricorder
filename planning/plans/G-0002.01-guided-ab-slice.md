## Hypothesis

One explicit-turn A/B sound investigation on the Tab5, guided by the Mac service (mock, then live), gives a useful, evidence-grounded comparison without the operator touching the Mac. The existing instrument code can support it and be hardened in place.

**Refuted if:** the operator needs developer help, guidance cites the wrong capture, changed settings mislead the comparison, network waits block local cancel, or the operator can't understand the result or pick a next action.

Out of scope: full Explore/Compare breadth, all sensors at once, streaming, calibrated acoustics, hosted deployment, full-duplex speech.

## Checks

1. [pass] Host tests cover the state sequence, stale/duplicate/wrong-session rejection, delay, cancel, disconnect and capacity limits.
2. [pass] Three mock handheld loops, each with two real captures, guidance, the recorded adjustment and a comparison joined to those captures.
3. [pass] Controlled trials: 5 s delayed reply, cancel while pending, service disconnect/reconnect. The UI stays live and old-session replies can't act.
4. [fail] **Three live-provider handheld loops** on the operator's own comparisons, with capture/transfer/inference/first-response timing and a repeat of A (A-to-A change beside B/A). The operator rates responsiveness and usefulness 1–5 (target ≥ 4), states the finding and names a next action unaided.
