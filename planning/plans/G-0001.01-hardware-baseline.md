# G-0001.01. Hardware baseline

Status: In progress
Goal: [G-0001](G-0001-trustworthy-live-investigation.md) — each built-in capability initializes reliably with known ownership.
ADRs: [0003](../adrs/ADR-0003-tab5-diagnostic-foundation.md), [0004](../adrs/ADR-0004-capture-completion.md), [0005](../adrs/ADR-0005-diagnostic-radio-startup.md)

## Hypothesis

A pinned native toolchain with one owner per peripheral can repeatedly initialize this Tab5's built-in capabilities, and the observed inputs/outputs are attributable to specific hardware.

**Refuted if:** a required peripheral fails repeatably, two stacks compete for one resource, detected hardware lacks a supported driver, or a clean rebuild does not reproduce startup.

## Checks

1. [pass] Inventory: record actual chip, panel, camera, flash/PSRAM and C6 firmware identity.
2. [open] Reproduction: two clean builds from pinned inputs boot; **10 cold starts and 10 software resets** with zero init failures.
3. [pass] Function: camera frame retained; mic capture + playback heard; touch reaches corners/center; IMU follows tilt; RTC advances; SD checksum round trip; Wi-Fi LAN round trip; physical mic mapping recorded.
4. [open] Ownership: each resource has one named owner; nothing is initialized twice.

Out of scope: concurrency (.02), calibration (.03), agent (.04), power/wake (.05). USB/RS-485 need fixtures and are deferred.

## Observed

- 2026-09-20 — [C1 pass] P4 v1.3, 16 MB flash, 32 MB PSRAM, ST7121 panel, C6 fw 1.4.1, BMI270 `0x24`, INA226 `0x5449`, camera PID `0xeb52` (SC202CS driver). Original flash backed up twice, hashes match. Evidence: `20260920-baseline/recovery.json` · `ec56685`.
- 2026-09-20 — [C2] Two clean factory builds boot; differences are timestamps only. BSP default display buffers abort in PPA; factory full-frame PSRAM buffers work. Evidence: `20260920-baseline` · `ec56685`. → ADR-0003
- 2026-09-20 — [C3] Touch (4 corners + center), tilt and RTC advance recorded. Evidence: `diagnostic-input-1` · `ec56685`.
- 2026-09-20 — [C3] Camera 1280×720 RGB565 frame and 3 s four-slot 48 kHz PCM exported with matching hashes after fixing a vendor G_FMT bug. Evidence: `media-boot-2` · `8c977d3`. → ADR-0004
- 2026-09-20 — Hosted radio startup aborted (TCM task stack, then early heap exhaustion). Fixed by deferring radio init and constraining task RAM. Evidence: `acoustic-network-3` · `ff8f7be`. → ADR-0005
- 2026-09-20 — [C3] LAN: three nonce echoes 263/35/39 ms. First joins failed until the correct network was chosen. Evidence: `lan-echo-1` · `0762e1f`.
- 2026-09-20 — [C3] Playback: phrase audible in raw slots 0/2 on speaker (faint) and both headphone ears; slots 1/3 static. Evidence: `473b62e`.
- 2026-09-20 — [C2] Software resets: the first run panicked from an LVGL startup race, fixed by holding the display lock. Ten `ESP_RST_SW` boots then passed. Evidence: `software-restarts-2` · `4e71bb2`, `82c43c8`.
- 2026-09-20 — [C3] Mic mapping: slot 2 ↔ hole near the USB connector, slot 0 ↔ farther hole (±0.7–2.8 dB margins, three takes each). Evidence: `audio-ingress-1/microphone-position.json` · `ffa4c2e`.
- 2026-09-20 — Vendor IMU helpers ignored Bosch errors; replaced with checked init/read (`imu_checked`). Evidence: `tests/test_imu_checked.py` · `6079c55`.
- 2026-09-22 — [C3 pass] SD card: write/readback checksum and LAN download pass; about 3.0 s per 1.15 MB capture. Evidence: `20260922-sd-testing` · `fbf930d`.
