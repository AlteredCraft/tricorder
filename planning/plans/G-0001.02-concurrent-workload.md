# G-0001.02. Sustain a responsive combined workload

Status: In progress
Goal: [G-0001 — a trustworthy live investigation](G-0001-trustworthy-live-investigation.md)
ADRs: [ADR-0007 — bounded camera FIFO](../adrs/ADR-0007-bounded-camera-fifo.md), [ADR-0008 — JPEG error-path fail-stop](../adrs/ADR-0008-jpeg-error-path-fail-stop.md), [ADR-0009 — owned preview and render placement](../adrs/ADR-0009-owned-preview-and-render-placement.md); broader concurrency choices remain under investigation.

## Proposal

### Hypothesis

The selected firmware can sustain camera preview, microphone acquisition and FFT, motion updates, touchscreen interaction and wireless transfer together without losing measurement samples or accumulating unbounded latency.

### Refuted if

At the declared workload, any audio sample gap is unexplained, capture loss is hidden, queues grow without bound, allocation fails, watchdog/reset occurs, or timing targets fail in repeated runs. Lowering resolution/rates is a dated proposal revision and a new run, not a retroactive pass.

### Build

1. Add counters, task/queue instrumentation and bounded buffers to the .01 diagnostic. Define ownership and overflow policies before running the combined test.
2. Establish isolated baselines, then progressively enable camera, audio/FFT, IMU, UI, networking and playback. Proposed starting profile: camera 640×480 at 15 frames/s if supported by the verified sensor/driver; 48 kHz signed 16-bit raw audio per available microphone channel; 2048-sample FFT at 20 updates/s; IMU at 100 Hz; animated UI at 30 frames/s. Record actual hardware rates and FFT window/scaling.
3. Add a receiver with configurable backpressure. Stream one mono raw-audio channel at 48 kHz/16-bit and 10 telemetry updates/s; upload one JPEG every 2 seconds. Play a short voice clip every 10 seconds in the playback phase. Preserve camera native format and all resampling/encoding settings in the manifest.
4. Run each isolated stage for 60 seconds, then three 10-minute combined runs, including 100 input events per run. Exercise mode changes and a 10-second receiver stall. Export raw counts as well as derived metrics.

### Out of scope

This tests the specified starting profile, not maximum camera resolution or every possible combination. Display callback timing is not physical touch-to-photon latency. Formal accuracy/calibration is .03; real model response quality is .04; battery endurance and wake are .05.

### How we know

Use the [shared evidence contract](G-0001.01-hardware-baseline.md#shared-run-evidence). Numeric limits are proposed acceptance targets, not verified performance.

1. Instrumentation: logs account for produced, consumed and dropped items by stream/sequence. Counters do not reset silently. Summary generation must report missing instrumentation as inconclusive.
2. Baseline: record achieved capture/presentation rates, p50/p95/max latency and CPU/memory usage per stage. Camera capture target is 15 frames/s with no unaccounted loss; display may repeat/drop previews only under an explicitly logged policy. FFT and IMU timestamps demonstrate target cadence.
3. Combined run: zero unexplained raw-audio sample gaps, allocation failures, watchdogs or crashes. Proposed acceptance: animated UI p95 frame interval ≤50 ms and no interval >200 ms; input-dispatch-to-display-submit p95 ≤100 ms across 100 events; no monotonic growth in queue occupancy.
4. Backpressure: queues remain within configured caps and local acquisition/UI keep their targets during the stall. Lossy remote-preview drops are counted; measurement loss is failure. After warmup, quiescent internal and external free-memory baselines return within 5% after repeated mode cycles, and each largest free block remains above the largest planned allocation. Record minima and stack margins; never report only average memory.

Cross-run comparison: join summaries by spec revision and workload, retain all runs including failures, and compare at least three repetitions before claiming stable behavior. Differences in hardware, gain, clock, driver, model, codec or workload remain explicit.
