# G-0001.02. Concurrent workload

Status: In progress
Goal: [G-0001](G-0001-trustworthy-live-investigation.md) — instruments stay responsive and lossless together.
ADRs: [0007](../adrs/ADR-0007-bounded-camera-fifo.md), [0008](../adrs/ADR-0008-jpeg-error-path-fail-stop.md), [0009](../adrs/ADR-0009-owned-preview-and-render-placement.md)
Revision: 2026-09-25: profile now matches the guided investigation, which records, then uploads, then plays guidance; it never streams audio or runs camera preview during a session. Continuous-stream and 30 fps preview load removed; C2 and C3 reworded to match (user-approved).

## Hypothesis

Across repeated guided investigations, the firmware keeps measurement audio lossless and the UI responsive at every step, with bounded queues and no memory growth.

**Refuted if:** there is an unexplained audio gap, hidden capture loss, unbounded queue growth, a failed allocation, a watchdog/reset, or timing targets fail on repeat. Lowering rates is a new run, not a retroactive pass.

## Checks

Profile (one session): a 1280×720 context photo; IMU steadiness at 25 Hz while waiting; 3 s captures (48 kHz, 4 slots, 16-bit) with live spectrum and a 60 ms UI refresh; upload in 4 KiB chunks after the microphone closes; spoken guidance streamed at 24 kHz mono and played at 48 kHz stereo.

1. [pass] Isolated 60 s baselines per stream, with produced/consumed/dropped counters.
2. [pass] **Three 10-minute runs** of back-to-back sessions with 100 input events each: zero unexplained audio gaps/allocation failures/crashes; UI frame interval p95 ≤ 50 ms, max ≤ 200 ms; input-to-submit p95 ≤ 100 ms; queues stay bounded.
3. [pass] A 10 s service stall during upload or guidance playback keeps the UI on target and ends in recovery or a visible failure, without reset. Retained captures stay intact.
4. [open] After repeated mode cycles, free memory returns to within 5% of the post-warmup baseline.
