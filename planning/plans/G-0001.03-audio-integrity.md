# G-0001.03. Preserve measurement audio while processing speech

Status: In progress
Goal: [G-0001 — a trustworthy live investigation](G-0001-trustworthy-live-investigation.md)
ADRs: None — architectural choices remain under investigation.
Date: 2026-09-20
Revision: Initial proposal; no hardware results.

## Proposal

### Hypothesis

A shared capture source can supply a trustworthy pre-enhancement measurement stream and a separately processed conversation stream without cross-mutation, hidden sample loss or ambiguity about playback contamination.

### Refuted if

Enabling speech processing changes the same captured measurement block's bytes, raw buffers are reused before consumption, physical microphone channels are misidentified, samples disappear without a counter, or derived measurement results cannot be reproduced from saved raw audio and settings.

### Build

1. Capture sequence-numbered PCM blocks with sample counters, physical-channel mapping, fixed gain, format, clipping flags and timestamps. Hash the ingress blocks before handing them to consumers.
2. Feed a separate speech-processing path; keep raw data immutable while referenced. Verify with deterministic PCM injection/replay before acoustic tests.
3. Compare device FFT results to an independent desktop reference using synthetic bin-centered tones, mixtures, silence and impulses. Record identical window, FFT normalization and sample-rate conventions.
4. Run an acoustic fixture with a stationary sound source, documented distance/orientation and fixed gain. Capture speech with a background tone, with/without speech processing and with/without device playback; repeat under .02 load.

### Out of scope

This does not certify absolute sound-pressure level, microphone frequency response or fault diagnosis. A calibrated reference instrument would be needed for those claims. Open-air A/B recordings are not expected to be byte-identical. Model speech understanding and conversational interruption belong to .04.

### How we know

Use the [shared evidence contract](G-0001.01-hardware-baseline.md#shared-run-evidence). Numeric limits are proposed acceptance targets, not verified performance.

1. Ingress: every saved raw block hash matches its ingress hash and sample counters remain continuous. Derived streams carry source ranges and processing configuration.
2. Deterministic replay: raw output is bit-identical with speech processing enabled/disabled. Saved data survives SD write/read checksum checks. No consumer retains a buffer after release.
3. DSP: proposed targets for non-clipped synthetic signals are dominant frequency within one FFT bin and normalized peak amplitude within 1% of the reference for a bin-centered tone; apply amplitude comparisons only above the documented noise/numerical floor. Store per-case error and failed fixtures.
4. Acoustic/load run: zero unaccounted raw-sample gaps, correct channel response and clipping counts, and logged speaker-active intervals. Report speech-path latency and background-tone attenuation without assuming a pass means calibrated measurement. Use a proposed 100 ms p95 local speech-processing budget. If speech and measurement cannot coexist within the load budget, record that constraint and test an explicit mode switch.

Cross-run comparison: join summaries by spec revision and workload, retain all runs including failures, and compare at least three repetitions before claiming stable behavior. Differences in hardware, gain, clock, driver, model, codec or workload remain explicit.
