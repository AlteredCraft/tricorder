# G-0001.03. Audio integrity

Status: In progress
Goal: [G-0001](G-0001-trustworthy-live-investigation.md) — measurement audio stays trustworthy while speech is processed.
ADRs: [0006](../adrs/ADR-0006-raw-audio-and-derived-speech.md)

## Hypothesis

One capture source can feed an unmodified measurement stream and a separately processed speech stream, with no cross-mutation, no hidden sample loss, and speaker contamination clearly identified.

**Refuted if:** speech processing changes raw bytes, raw buffers are reused while referenced, mic channels are misidentified, samples vanish without a counter, or measurements can't be reproduced from saved raw audio.

## Checks

1. [pass] Ingress: saved raw block hashes match their ingress hashes; sample counters are continuous.
2. [pass] Replay: raw output is bit-identical whether speech processing is on or off.
3. [pass] DSP: on synthetic fixtures, device FFT peak is within one bin and within 1% amplitude of an independent desktop DFT.
4. [open] Acoustic/load: under .02 load, zero unaccounted gaps, speaker-active intervals logged, speech path p95 ≤ 100 ms. If speech and measurement can't coexist, test an explicit mode switch.

Out of scope: calibrated SPL, mic frequency response, fault diagnosis.
