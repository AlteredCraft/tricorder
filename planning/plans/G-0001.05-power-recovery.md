# G-0001.05. Expose energy cost and recover predictably

Status: Not built
Goal: G-0001 — a trustworthy live investigation
Date: 2026-09-20
Revision: Initial proposal; no hardware results.

## Proposal

### Hypothesis

The selected stack can expose this unit's power behavior and preserve a usable investigation across supported sleep/wake, disconnect and interruption scenarios without corrupting previously completed evidence.

### Refuted if

Power readings have unexplained units/sign/source, wake repeatedly fails, acquisition uses stale handles after resume, completed captures become unreadable, or charging/power behavior depends on undocumented initialization.

### Build

1. Instrument INA226-derived readings and relevant charging/external-power state through the chosen board driver. Record calibration constants, sign convention, sampling cadence and which rail is being measured.
2. Measure screen-only, camera, audio/DSP, wireless-transfer and combined .02/.04 profiles at controlled brightness and supply conditions. Record available internal temperatures by source; do not label them ambient.
3. Exercise supported touch, motion and timed wake separately with explicit state transitions. Run software restarts and service disconnects during sessions, preserving capture metadata and sequence/boot identity.
4. Verify file finalization/recovery using disposable test captures and controlled interruption. Recover a partially written current capture explicitly while checking earlier completed captures. Observe battery operation for a defined duration; use external power for long tests if needed.

### Out of scope

No battery-runtime promise is made in advance. Low-power coprocessor firmware, exhaustive battery aging/thermal tests and certification are deferred. Physical battery removal and charging procedures follow the board vendor's guidance; do not deliberately deep-discharge the battery.

### How we know

Use the [shared evidence contract](../stack-validation.md#evidence-contract-for-the-experiments). Numeric limits are proposed acceptance targets, not verified performance.

1. Telemetry: power logs identify source/units and agree qualitatively with controlled load changes. If a reference meter is used, compare the same rail and account for conversion losses; otherwise absolute accuracy remains Not tested.
2. Profiles: collect three 2-minute runs per fixed profile after warmup. Report average/peak measured power and energy over time, plus configuration. Whole-system runtime is measured by elapsed battery operation; a short-run extrapolation is labeled an estimate with assumptions.
3. Wake: 20 cycles per supported wake mode, zero failed resumptions, and proposed p95 wake-event-to-instrument-ready ≤2 seconds. If the wake source has no observable timestamp, use an instrumented external trigger or mark that latency inconclusive. All resumed streams receive current boot/session metadata.
4. Recovery: 10 controlled interruption trials. Earlier finalized capture hashes remain intact; incomplete captures are marked incomplete and never cited by the agent as complete. No duplicate IDs, silently truncated files or unbounded recovery loops.
5. Handheld session: retain at least one 30-minute representative battery run if capacity permits, recording start/end conditions and operator comfort notes. Record actual duration if it stops early; do not turn incomplete endurance evidence into a pass.

Cross-run comparison: join summaries by spec revision and workload, retain all runs including failures, and compare at least three repetitions before claiming stable behavior. Differences in hardware, gain, clock, driver, model, codec or workload remain explicit.
