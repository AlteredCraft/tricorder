# G-0001.05. Power and recovery

Status: Not built
Goal: [G-0001](G-0001-trustworthy-live-investigation.md) — energy cost is visible and investigations survive interruptions.
ADRs: None

## Hypothesis

The stack can report this unit's power use and keep an investigation usable across supported sleep/wake, disconnect and interruption, without corrupting completed evidence.

**Refuted if:** power readings have unexplained units/sign/source, wake fails repeatably, resumed acquisition uses stale handles, completed captures become unreadable, or power behavior depends on undocumented initialization.

## Checks

1. Telemetry: INA226 readings have stated rail, units and sign, and track controlled load changes.
2. Profiles: three 2-minute runs each for screen-only, camera, audio/DSP, Wi-Fi and combined; report average/peak power.
3. Wake: 20 cycles per supported wake source, zero failures, wake-to-ready p95 ≤ 2 s.
4. Recovery: 10 controlled interruptions; finalized captures keep their hashes, and incomplete ones are marked incomplete and never cited.
5. One 30-minute handheld battery session with comfort notes.

Out of scope: runtime promises, LP-core firmware, battery aging/thermal tests.
